  • title :
    Global Sea Ice Drift Climate Data Record Version 1 from the EUMETSAT OSI SAF
    summary :
    This climate data record of sea ice drift vectors is obtained from coarse resolution passive microwave satellite data (SSM/I, SSMIS, AMSR-E and AMSR2) over the polar regions (Arctic and Antarctic). The processing chain features: 1) the continuous maximum cross-correlation (CMCC) algorithm, 2) use of a wind-driven free-drift model in the summer melt season, 3) computation of per-pixel uncertainties, and 4) both single-sensor and multi-sensor drift fields. This dataset was generated by the EUMETSAT Ocean and Sea Ice Satellite Application Facility (OSI SAF).
